RIA PANERI RAVAL

My Name is Ria

I’m a results-driven Services Analyst with over 6 years of experience specializing in data analysis, BI dashboarding, and financial reporting. I hold a postgraduate degree (M.Tech) in Computer Software from B.H. Gardi College of Engineering, Gujarat.

Currently, I work as a Senior Associate – Projects at Borderless Access, where I lead end-to-end reporting implementations using Power BI, Tableau, and Excel. My responsibilities include building interactive dashboards, performing variance and trend analysis, and transforming business KPIs into actionable insights for global clients.

Previously, I served as a Technical SEO & Reporting Analyst at AnandaVak, where I managed WordPress-based websites, optimized technical SEO, and developed performance dashboards using Google Analytics and Google Data Studio. Earlier in my career at EYUVA Technologies, I focused on on-page SEO, user engagement optimization, and visual dashboard reporting.

With hands-on experience in tools like Tableau, Power BI, Microsoft Excel, Google Analytics, and Figma, I combine technical skills with a keen eye for business performance. I’m passionate about turning data into clear, decision-ready insights that add measurable value to organizations.

Skills include Google Analytics, Tableau, Power BI, Excel, CRM, and dashboard visualization.
