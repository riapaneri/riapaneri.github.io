About Me

I’m a results-driven Services Analyst with over 6 years of experience specializing in data analysis, BI dashboarding, and financial reporting. I hold an M.Tech in Computer Software from B.H. Gardi College of Engineering, Gujarat.

Currently, I work as a Senior Associate – Projects at Borderless Access, leading end-to-end reporting implementations using Power BI, Tableau, and Excel. I build interactive dashboards, perform variance and trend analysis, and translate business KPIs into decision-ready insights for global clients.

Previously, I worked as a Technical SEO & Reporting Analyst at AnandaVak and began my career at EYUVA Technologies focusing on on-page SEO and dashboard-driven reporting. I combine analytical rigor with stakeholder communication to deliver actionable outcomes.

Key tools: Tableau • Power BI • Excel (advanced) • Google Analytics • Figma • WordPress
